How it works

Salt, water, power. Then seven products.

An ion-exchange membrane is the core of the plant. It lets only sodium ions pass, so chlorine forms on one side and pure caustic soda and hydrogen form on the other. The process uses no mercury and no asbestos.

2NaCl+2H₂O→2NaOH+Cl₂+H₂
  1. Brine preparationWe dissolve Afar salt and purify it to parts-per-billion hardness with ion-exchange resins.
  2. Membrane electrolysisDirect current splits the brine. Chlorine forms at the anode, and caustic soda and hydrogen form at the cathode.
  3. Caustic finishingEvaporators concentrate the lye from 32% to 50%, and a flaker turns part of it into 98% flakes.
  4. Chlorine processingChlorine is dried, compressed and liquefied, or sent to the HCl, hypochlorite and coagulant units.
  5. Hydrogen recoveryHydrogen goes into HCl synthesis and fuels the boilers. Nothing is vented as waste.
